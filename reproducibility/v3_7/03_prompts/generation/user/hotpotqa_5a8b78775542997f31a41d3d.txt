He went out in search of employment to the upper regions of western ghats and worked as a mercenary trooper under various Maratha generals.  According to Kincaid & Parasnis, Balaji Vishwanath entered the Maratha administration during the reign of Chhatrapati Sambhaji or the regency of his brother, Rajaram. Later he served as an accountant for the Maratha general, Dhanaji Jadhav, at Janjira.  Between 1699 and 1702, he served as the "Sar-subhedar" or head-administrator at Pune and from 1704 to 1707 as Sarsubedar of Daulatabad.
